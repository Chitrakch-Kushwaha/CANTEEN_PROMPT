# ⚡ QUICK PROMPT --- COLLEGE CANTEEN CHALLENGE

## 1. ROLE

Act as an expert college-canteen strategist, food entrepreneur, and demand-forecasting analyst. Your objective is to design a realistic, data-driven, and executable one-week operations plan that balances student affordability, operational speed, profit margins, and food-waste reduction.

## 2. CHALLENGE OVERVIEW

You have a strict total budget of ₹10,000 to optimize and run a college canteen for 7 days. Your target demographic consists of college students characterized by:

* Limited disposable income

* Short intervals between classes

* High-volume traffic during concentrated peak hours

* Diverse food preferences (seeking affordable, tasty, filling, and fast options)

Determine the menu, preparation quantities, pricing strategy, precise ₹10,000 budget allocation, and dynamic demand-response tactics.

## 3. PRIMARY GOALS

Your output must achieve the following:

* Maintain aggressive affordability for students while generating a sustainable net profit.

* Deploy the ₹10,000 budget with maximum capital efficiency.

* Minimize end-of-day food wastage to near zero.

* Eliminate bottlenecks to reduce queue times during peak hours.

* Prevent stock-outs of high-demand items without over-complicating inventory.

* **Crucial Guideline:** Do not default to high prices for high profits. Demonstrate a sophisticated balance of low margin/high volume, strategic pricing, and student satisfaction.

## 4. KEY ASSUMPTIONS

Before generating the plan, explicitly list realistic baseline assumptions for the following variables. Do not present these estimates as absolute facts; label them clearly as operational assumptions:

* Daily active canteen users (footfall)

* Conversion rate (percentage of footfall making a purchase)

* Specific peak hours

* Average order value (spending capacity per student)

* Wholesale ingredient costs

* Kitchen preparation and storage capacity

* Expected day-to-day demand variance

* Operating days (7 days)

## 8. DEMAND FORECASTING

Segment and estimate demand across distinct daily phases: Morning Break, Lunch/Major Break, Afternoon, and End-of-Day. Categorize menu items by velocity (High, Medium, Low Demand) and explain the batch-preparation strategy for each. Use this inventory formula: *Required Stock = Expected Demand + Safety Stock − Existing Inventory* Define your chosen Safety Stock percentage and justify its necessity based on spoilage risk vs. stock-out risk.

## 9. PEAK-HOUR MANAGEMENT

Design a high-throughput operational system to handle sudden student surges:

* **Pre-Peak:** Advance prep, pre-portioning, chilling beverages, and optimizing the checkout flow.

* **During Peak:** Prioritization of fast-serve items, combo-driven fast lanes, queue management, and accessibility of top sellers.

* **Post-Peak:** Immediate variance analysis (Actual vs. Expected Sales) to adjust the next prep cycle.

## 10. DEMAND SHOCK PROTOCOLS

Detail tactical responses for the following scenarios:

* **+30% Demand Spike:** How to reallocate stock, prioritize items, and deploy emergency reserves.

* **-30% Demand Drop:** How to halt prep, safely repurpose leftover ingredients, and utilize flash discounts/combos to clear perishable stock.

* **Flagship Stock-Out:** Alternative cross-selling tactics, customer communication, and next-day inventory adjustments.

## 11. WASTE-MINIMIZATION SYSTEM

Develop a strict zero-waste protocol incorporating:

* Small-batch, just-in-time preparation.

* Strict FIFO (First-In, First-Out) inventory rotation.

* Safe, planned cross-utilization of ingredients across multiple menu items.

* End-of-day flash sales or bundling for perishables.

* *Note:* Do not suggest compromising food safety or extending shelf life beyond health standards.

## 13. FINANCIAL ANALYSIS

Provide a rigorous, mathematically sound financial breakdown:

* **Total Revenue:** Σ (Units Sold × Selling Price)

* **Total Variable Cost:** Σ (Units Sold × Cost per Unit)

* **Gross Profit:** Total Revenue − Total Variable Cost

* **Overall Profit Margin:** (Gross Profit ÷ Total Revenue) × 100

* **Break-Even Point:** Calculate the exact number of units/average orders required to recoup the ₹10,000 outlay.

## 14. WHAT-IF SCENARIO ANALYSIS

## 16. CREATIVE LOW-COST GROWTH HACK
